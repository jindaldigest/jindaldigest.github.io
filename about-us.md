​The Jindal Digest for Competition and Innovation Laws is a student edited, interdisciplinary, legal blog which aims to provide an expansive view of the contemporary developments in antitrust, intellectual property and technology law. Through this forum, we aim to fill in a critical lacuna by providing accessible, yet quality-driven literature focusing on the rapid legal developments in this sphere. We are excited to invite our readers to join us in this journey with their contributions.
