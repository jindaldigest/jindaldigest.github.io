The Jindal Digest for Competition and Innovation Laws (JDCIL) is a student-edited, interdisciplinary legal blog that serves as a platform for in-depth analysis and discussion of contemporary developments in antitrust, intellectual property, and technology law. Our mission is to bridge the gap between academic research and practical legal developments in these dynamic fields.

## Our Vision

We aim to:
- Provide accessible yet academically rigorous analysis of legal developments
- Foster meaningful discussions on competition and innovation law
- Create a platform for students and professionals to share insights
- Contribute to the broader legal discourse in India and globally

## Our Focus Areas

1. **Competition Law**
   - Antitrust regulations and enforcement
   - Market competition and consumer welfare
   - Merger control and market analysis
   - Competition policy and reforms

2. **Innovation Law**
   - Intellectual property rights
   - Technology transfer and licensing
   - Patent law and innovation policy
   - Digital rights and innovation

3. **Technology Law**
   - Data protection and privacy
   - Digital markets and regulation
   - E-commerce and consumer protection
   - Emerging technologies and legal frameworks

## Our Team

JDCIL is run by a dedicated team of students from Jindal Global Law School, who work under the guidance of faculty members and industry experts. Our editorial board ensures the quality and relevance of all published content.

## Get Involved

We welcome contributions from:
- Law students
- Legal practitioners
- Academic researchers
- Industry experts
- Policy makers
